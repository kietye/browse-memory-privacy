中文

一句话：浏览记忆收集的所有数据都只保存在你这台电脑的浏览器里，扩展不向任何服务器发送数据，开发者也看不到你的任何数据。

我们收集哪些数据，用来做什么

数据何时收集用途
浏览过的网址、网页标题、访问时间与次数安装后导入最近 90 天的浏览历史；之后每次访问网页让你在扩展里搜索、找回浏览过的网页
网页正文片段仅在你主动开启“全文记忆”并授权后，读取你打开且停留 5 秒以上的网页让你按正文内容找回网页，并在结果中显示相关摘要
搜索索引（由标题和正文计算出的数字向量）在本机由上述数据计算得出支持“按意思”搜索
扩展设置（暂停状态、不记录的网站名单、全文开关）你修改设置时按你的选择控制记录范围

你在扩展里输入的搜索词只在本机用于本次搜索，不会被保存或发送。

数据存在哪里

所有数据都保存在你浏览器的本机存储（IndexedDB）中。按意思搜索所用的文本模型随扩展一起安装，在你的电脑上运行。扩展运行时不发起任何网络请求，不使用分析、统计、广告或任何第三方服务。

我们不做的事

  • 不上传、不出售、不转让你的任何数据；
  • 不把数据用于广告、信用评估或与搜索浏览记录无关的任何用途；
  • 不在无痕窗口中运行；
  • 默认不记录银行、支付、邮箱、网盘、医疗问诊等网站，以及内网地址的正文和标题；你可以在设置中增删“不记录的网站”。

你的控制权

  • 暂停记录：1 小时或直到手动恢复；
  • 不记录某个网站：在设置或工具栏图标右键菜单中添加，已有记录会同时删除；
  • 删除单条记录：在搜索结果中删除；
  • 导出：在设置页导出 JSON；
  • 清空：在设置页一键清空全部记忆；
  • 关闭全文记忆：在设置页关闭，或在浏览器扩展管理中撤销网站访问权限；
  • 卸载扩展：浏览器会删除扩展保存的全部本机数据。

数据保留

数据保留在你的电脑上，直到你删除、清空或卸载扩展。

Chrome 应用商店“有限使用”声明

浏览记忆对从 Google API 获取的信息（包括浏览历史）的使用与传输，遵守 Chrome 应用商店用户数据政策，包括其中的“有限使用”（Limited Use）要求。

政策变更

如果数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策并修改“最后更新”日期；涉及新增数据收集时，会在扩展内提示并再次征得你的同意。

English

In short: everything Browse Memory collects stays in your browser on this device. The extension sends no data to any server, and the developer cannot see any of your data.

What we collect and why

DataWhenPurpose
URLs, page titles, visit times and countsOn install (the last 90 days of history) and on each later visitLet you search for and return to pages you have visited
Page text excerptsOnly after you turn on "Full-text memory" and grant permission, for pages you open and keep open for 5+ secondsLet you find pages by their content and show matching excerpts
Search index (numeric vectors computed from titles and text)Computed locally from the data aboveSearch by meaning
Settings (paused state, sites not to record, full-text switch)When you change themApply your choices

Search queries are used locally for that search only; they are not stored or sent anywhere.

Where data is stored

All data is stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB). The text model used for meaning-based search ships with the extension and runs on your device. The extension makes no network requests and uses no analytics, advertising, or third-party services.

What we never do

  • We do not upload, sell, or transfer your data.
  • We do not use it for advertising, creditworthiness, or any purpose unrelated to searching your browsing history.
  • The extension does not run in incognito windows.
  • By default it does not record banking, payment, email, cloud storage, or medical consultation sites, or private-network addresses. You can edit this list in Settings.

Your controls

Pause recording; exclude a site (existing records are deleted); delete a single result; export as JSON; clear all memory; turn off full-text memory or revoke site access; uninstalling the extension removes all of its local data.

Retention

Data stays on your device until you delete it, clear it, or uninstall the extension.

Limited Use disclosure

Browse Memory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs, including browsing history, adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

We will update this policy and its date if our practices change, and ask for your consent again in the extension before any new data collection.
